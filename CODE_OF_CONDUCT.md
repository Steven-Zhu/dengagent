# Community conduct

Treat participants respectfully. Discuss ideas and evidence without harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual content or disclosure of private information. Respect requests to stop an interaction. Use accessible, constructive language in issues, reviews and discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, lock discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation to keep the project usable and safe. Responses should be proportionate and explain the relevant conduct when doing so would not disclose private information.

Use repository moderation/reporting controls to flag misconduct. Do not reproduce private information in public reports. If direct private contact is needed and no channel exists, ask a maintainer to establish one before sharing details. Security reports follow SECURITY.md. Maintainers are expected to follow the same conduct and respect confidentiality.

## 中文说明

尊重参与者，以事实和建设性意见讨论。不得骚扰、歧视、威胁、传播不受欢迎的性内容或披露隐私；尊重停止互动的请求。

维护者可按比例移除不当内容、锁定讨论、拒绝贡献或限制参与，并在不泄露隐私的前提下说明原因。维护者同样遵守此准则并尊重保密。

使用仓库举报或管理入口反映问题，不在公开报告中重复隐私内容。需要私下沟通而没有渠道时，先请求维护者建立私密渠道。安全问题使用 [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) 的流程。
